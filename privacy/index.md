# 3Mimic Privacy Policy

**Effective date: November 13, 2026**

3Mimic is an English-learning application that helps users improve their English through short video clips, captions, and learning activities.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how you can request deletion of your account and associated personal data.

## 1. Information We Collect

When you use 3Mimic, we may collect and process the following information:

### Account information
- Email address.
- Name and profile photo provided through Google Sign-In.
- Google account identifier used to authenticate your account.

### Learning information
- Your native language.
- The language you are learning.
- Your selected learning level.
- Placement test answers and results.
- Learning progress and activity.
- Saved words and favorite video clips.

### App content
3Mimic stores learning content such as video identifiers, captions, and related educational information in its database. This content is not necessarily personal information associated with an individual user.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use account and learning information to:
- Create and authenticate your account.
- Personalize your learning experience.
- Save your language preferences and learning level.
- Store placement test results and learning progress.
- Provide learning activities and saved content.
- Respond to support and privacy requests.

## 3. Google Sign-In

3Mimic uses Google Sign-In to authenticate users.

When you sign in, Google provides information needed to authenticate your account, which may include your email address, name, profile photo, and Google account identifier.

We do not collect or store your Google password.

Google's processing of information is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

## 4. YouTube Videos and Third-Party Services

3Mimic uses the YouTube embedded player to display video clips within the application.

When the YouTube player loads or a video is played, YouTube/Google may receive information such as device and connection information, IP address, and information about video playback and interactions.

YouTube processes this information under its own terms and privacy policy. 3Mimic does not control YouTube's independent data practices.

You can review:
- YouTube Terms of Service: https://www.youtube.com/t/terms
- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

3Mimic stores its own learning captions and related educational information in its application database.

## 5. Data Storage and Hosting

3Mimic processes account and learning information through its backend and database infrastructure hosted on Railway.

Information is stored and processed as needed to provide the application's features.

## 6. Advertising and Analytics

3Mimic does not integrate third-party advertising services or third-party analytics and crash-reporting tools such as Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, or Google AdMob.

However, videos are provided through YouTube, which may display advertisements or process information according to Google's and YouTube's own policies and settings.

We do not sell users' personal information.

## 7. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect user information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

Communication between the application and our backend is protected using HTTPS.

No internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

## 8. Data Retention and Account Deletion

We retain account information and associated learning records while an account remains active.

You can request account deletion:

**Inside the application:** Open Settings, select Delete Account, and confirm.

**Outside the application:** Visit our Account Deletion page or email us at:

ivcddvhd@gmail.com

After a deletion request is verified and completed, we delete the user's account and associated personal learning records from our active application database.

Shared educational content, such as general video captions, may remain because it is not personal account data.

If you contact us by email, we may ask for reasonable information to verify that you own the account.

## 9. Children's Privacy

3Mimic is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable privacy laws.

If you believe a child has provided personal information improperly, contact us so we can review the request.

## 10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our application or data practices change.

The latest version will be published on this page with its effective date.
