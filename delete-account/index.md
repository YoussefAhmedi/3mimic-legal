# 3Mimic – Account and Data Deletion

3Mimic allows users to request deletion of their accounts and associated personal learning data.

You do not need to have the application installed to submit a deletion request.

## Option 1: Request Deletion by Email

Send an email to:

**ivcddvhd@gmail.com**

Use this subject:

**3Mimic Account Deletion Request**

Include:
- The email address associated with your 3Mimic account.
- A statement confirming that you want your account and associated personal data permanently deleted.

Whenever possible, send your request from the email address associated with your 3Mimic account so that we can verify ownership.

Do not send your Google password or authentication tokens.

We will review your request, verify account ownership, and complete the deletion as soon as reasonably possible. We may contact you if additional information is needed.

## Option 2: Delete Your Account Inside the App

If you still have 3Mimic installed:

1. Open the application.
2. Go to Settings.
3. Select Delete Account.
4. Confirm the deletion.

## What Information Is Deleted?

After a deletion request is verified and completed, we delete the account and associated personal learning records, including:

- Account information and profile.
- Language preferences.
- Learning level.
- Placement test answers and results.
- Learning progress.
- Saved words.
- Favorite video clips.
- Other personal learning records associated with the account.

General educational content shared across users, such as video captions and video identifiers, is not deleted when an individual account is deleted because it is not personal account data.
